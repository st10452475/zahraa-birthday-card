Happy Birthday

happy birthday to my favourite person in the whole world ❤️

it's crazy to think that someone as special as you exists, and somehow i get to call you my girl. i don't think i'll ever stop being grateful for having you in my life because meeting you changed everything for me. before you, i never knew a person could become my home, but with you, no matter how far apart we are, i always feel like i'm exactly where i'm supposed to be.

you've brought so much peace into my life without even trying. you've taught me what it feels like to be understood, cared for, and loved in a way that's so genuine. every conversation, every laugh, every random voice note, every little moment we've shared has become a memory i hold close to my heart. even the normal days become my favourite because they're spent with you.

what i admire most about you isn't just how beautiful you are, even though i could spend forever talking about that. it's your heart. the way you care so deeply about the people you love, the way you always give your all, the way you stay strong even when life gets difficult. you inspire me more than you'll ever realise, and you make me want to become a better man every single day.

my favourite dream is the one where i get to spend the rest of my life with you. i can't wait for the day distance is no longer part of our story, when birthdays mean waking up next to you, stealing the first hug of the day, and reminding you in person how loved you are.

until then, i'll keep choosing you every single day, just like i always will.

thank you for being my safe place, my biggest blessing, my best friend, and the greatest love i've ever known.

happy birthday, my zahraa.

i love you more than you'll ever truly know, always and forever ❤️
